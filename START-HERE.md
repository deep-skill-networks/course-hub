# Start here

Your path through the program, step by step. It uses week numbers; your batch timings are on the [program page](https://sanjaynegi.in/programs/), and your live session links and dates are on the learning platform.

## Where things live

| Place | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **The learning platform** ([learn.sanjaynegi.in](https://learn.sanjaynegi.in)) | **WORKSHOPS:** your live sessions and their Zoom links. **COURSES:** session recordings, the week's videos and notes, and links to the weekly quizzes |
| **GitHub** (`deep-skill-networks` org) | Do the labs and your capstone in **your own private repositories**, open pull requests, get feedback and grades |
| **This repository** (`course-hub`) | Read the syllabus, setup guides, policies, glossary, radar and case library |

Your own repositories are created for you, named `<course-repo>-<cohort>-<your-github-username>`; for example, `prework-ml-foundations-cohort-2027-01-asha-dev`. Find them under your GitHub invitations, or in the `deep-skill-networks` organisation's repository list once you've accepted.

## Step 1 — Before pre-work opens (about 1 hour)
1. Sign in to the [learning platform](https://learn.sanjaynegi.in) with the phone number you enrolled with (you'll get a one-time code). Check that you see your sessions under **WORKSHOPS** and the course under **COURSES**.
2. Send your GitHub username when you enrol, and **accept the invitation** to the `deep-skill-networks` organisation.
3. Read [setup/prerequisites.md](setup/prerequisites.md): accounts, machine, tools.
4. Read [setup/api-keys-and-costs.md](setup/api-keys-and-costs.md): how to keep spending near ₹0.
5. Create an **AWS account** with a billing alert (the capstone deploys to AWS).
6. Read the [assessment policy](assessments/policy.md), so you know how grading, extensions and AI-tool use work.

## Step 2 — Pre-work (2–3 weeks before week 1)
Do these in order, in your own copies:

| Order | Repository | What you do | Ends with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `prework-infra-bootcamp` (P1) | Labs P1.1–P1.4: Docker, CI, Terraform on AWS, Kubernetes | **P1 gate** |
| 2 | `prework-ml-foundations` (P2) | Labs P2.1–P2.2: baselines, honest metrics | **P2 gate** (the leakage trap) |
| 3 | your capstone repository | Follow its README and `docs/deploy-aws.md` | Milestone **`ms0`** |

Each repository's README tells you how to open it in a dev container and which lab comes first. The two gates and `ms0` are due at the end of **week 2**, because pre-work spans the year-end holidays. Starting early makes week 1 far easier.

## Step 3 — Every week of the 16 (7–9 hours)

| When | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Before your first session of the week | Learning platform → COURSES | Watch the week's short videos or notes; answer the check questions |
| Your batch's live sessions | Learning platform → WORKSHOPS (Zoom); recordings appear under COURSES | Concepts, design exercises and a failure clinic, with your pod |
| During the week | Your module lab repository on GitHub | Do the week's lab; open a pull request; the **Feedback** check runs on every push |
| During the week | Learning platform → COURSES | Take the weekly quiz: two attempts, best counts, explanations on submit |
| Every two weeks | Your capstone repository | Open the milestone pull request (`ms1`–`ms8`); your pod-mate reviews it |
| Sunday 23:59 IST after the week | — | Lab, quiz and any milestone due |

**Submitting:** open a pull request, add the label `submit`, and put one line in the description about any significant AI help. The course grader comments your result on the pull request, usually within an hour. You can push fixes and resubmit until the deadline.

**Falling behind?** Use your one no-questions-asked extension per course (form linked on the learning platform), watch the recording, and ask in your batch channel. If you miss a deadline you'll get an automatic reminder, then a nudge from your pod buddy. That's support, not a penalty.

## Step 4 — Weeks 15–16
A **game day** on your own deployed system, then your **capstone defense**: a recorded client pitch plus a live Q&A and incident. See the [syllabus](syllabus/cohort-16-week.md).

## Step 5 — After the course
The **alumni year**: fortnightly sessions for 12 months covering architecture reviews, incident teardowns, radar briefings and demos. You may copy your capstone to your own GitHub account and make it public for your portfolio.
